How to Delete Your Account

Under Google Play's User Data policy and global privacy standards (GDPR/CCPA), you have full control over your data. We provide two easy methods to permanently delete your account and personal information:

📱 Method 1: In-App (Instant)

If you currently have the WeTravel app installed on your device:

  1. Open the WeTravel app and log in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab (bottom right).
  3. Tap Account Settings (gear icon).
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete Account in red.
  5. Confirm by tapping Yes, Delete My Account.

• Processed immediately in real-time.

🌐 Method 2: Web Request (No App Needed)

If you have uninstalled the app or lost access to your device:

Fill out the official deletion request form below using the email registered with your WeTravel account. Our automated system will log your request and verify your deletion.

• Processed within 14–30 business days.
